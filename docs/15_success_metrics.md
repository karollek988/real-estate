# Success Metrics

**Date:** 2026-07-13 · **Sprint:** 3

## Primary KPIs (does the MVP deliver real value?)

- **Verdict-to-evidence click-through rate** — % of users who click into
  the "why" panel after seeing a verdict. Low click-through suggests
  either total trust (good) or total disengagement (bad) — must be read
  alongside qualitative feedback, not alone.
- **Return rate with a *different* listing** — a user pasting a second,
  unrelated listing URL is the strongest behavioral signal that the
  first verdict was trusted enough to use again for a live decision.
- **Session-to-decision proximity** — self-reported (short optional
  prompt: "are you currently bidding on this?") — tells us whether we're
  reaching people at the moment of real stakes, not just browsing.

## Trust metrics (the core differentiator from Hemnet/Booli)

- **Verdict agreement rate** — for a sample of objects, do independent
  human reviewers (or, later, actual sale outcomes) agree with our
  price-fair/overpriced call? This is the single most important metric
  long-term, since the whole positioning rests on being *right*, not
  just fast.
- **"We don't know" honesty rate** — % of objects where we correctly
  flagged insufficient data rather than forcing a verdict. A product
  that always has an opinion, even on thin data, will eventually be
  caught being wrong and lose the trust the positioning depends on.

## Engagement metrics (standard, secondary to trust)

- Returning users (week-over-week)
- Objects analyzed per session
- Time from paste-URL to verdict-viewed (should be seconds, not minutes)

## Metrics to explicitly avoid over-indexing on

- **Time spent on site** — a listing portal wants this; we don't. A fast,
  correct verdict that ends the session in 90 seconds is a win, not a
  failure, and optimizing for session length would pull the product
  toward becoming the marketplace we've explicitly said we won't be (see
  [[11_product_positioning]]).
- **Number of listings covered** — breadth is a distraction from depth
  and correctness in the MVP phase; covering fewer objects with a
  trustworthy verdict beats covering many with a shaky one.

## Longer-term / post-MVP metrics (Future Vision territory)

- Conversion to any paid tier, once one exists
- Verdict accuracy tracked against actual realized sale prices over time
  (only possible once enough tracked objects have sold)
