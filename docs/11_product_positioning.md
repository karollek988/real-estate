# Product Positioning

**Date:** 2026-07-13 · **Sprint:** 3

## One sentence

We turn the scattered, technical evidence buried in Swedish government
registers and BRF filings into a plain-language verdict — fair price,
financial risk, and future outlook — for a specific property, in under a
minute, before you bid.

## Mission

Give every buyer the analytical depth that today only professionals and
insiders have, so no one bids blind on the largest purchase of their
life.

## Vision

A Sweden where "was this a fair price, and was this BRF financially
sound" is a question anyone can answer in seconds, not a question only
answerable in hindsight or with a professional's help.

## Value proposition

For a specific listing, we synthesize public registers, BRF filings, and
market data no ordinary buyer has the time or expertise to read, into
three things a buyer actually needs before they bid:

1. **Is the asking price fair, and what should I actually expect to pay?**
2. **Is this BRF (or this house) financially/structurally sound, or is
   there a hidden risk?**
3. **Is this area's trajectory (schools, transit, safety, prices) going
   up or down?**

## What makes us fundamentally different from Hemnet/Booli

Hemnet and Booli are **discovery** products — they help you find
listings that match filters, and they are (correctly) optimized for
broad reach and broker relationships, because they are marketplaces.
We are not a marketplace and we do not want to be one.

We are an **analysis layer that sits downstream of discovery**. A user
finds a listing on Hemnet, then brings it to us to get the verdict
Hemnet has no incentive to give — because Hemnet's revenue comes from
brokers listing objects, not from telling a buyer "this is overpriced"
or "this BRF's finances are concerning." Our incentive is aligned with
the buyer, not the seller's agent. That is the moat: not more listings,
not better search filters, but trustworthy analysis a listing portal
structurally cannot offer without conflicting with its own business
model.

Concretely, we will never:
- Aggregate our own listings inventory as the core product.
- Take payment from brokers/sellers for placement or visibility.
- Optimize for "time spent browsing listings" as a success metric.

We will always:
- Be explicit about what we don't know / can't verify for a given object
  (Sweden has genuine data gaps — e.g. no bostadsrätt sold-price
  register exists at all).
- Show our reasoning, not just a score (see [[12_user_journey]] and the
  betting project's `ExplanationEngine`/customer-report precedent, which
  we mirror in spirit here — never a black-box number without a "why").
